How billing works

Three layers. One invoice.

Every invoice and every pricing page shows the same three layers. No blended charges, no mystery line items.

1

SUBSCRIPTION

The plan price and what allowance is included, in plain units — minutes, agents, swarms. Flat recurring charge every month.

2

USAGE

Metered, transparent, shown by product. Packs offered before any hard stop. Warned at 50%, 80%, 100% — then you choose to add or pause.

3

SERVICES

One-time or retainer, scoped in its own statement of work. Never blended into the subscription line. Always a separate agreement.

We say
  • Included allowance, then packs
  • Growth swarm, no guaranteed result
  • Warn before any hard stop
  • Retries and errors are on us
We never say
  • Unlimited — anywhere, ever
  • Anything implying a promised outcome or ROI
  • Free without a clear limit
  • Just in front of a price
