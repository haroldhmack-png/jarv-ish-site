This page documents current technical behavior. It requires legal review before public launch.

Last updated July 18, 2026. This is a pre-launch product draft, not a final counsel-approved policy.

Current website behavior

The static site does not use a configured lead endpoint by default. When the endpoint is blank, form data is not transmitted or persisted. The demo confirmation explicitly states this.

When lead submission is enabled

An approved private endpoint may receive the information you submit: name, email, company, role, agent framework, plan or product interest, team size, and notes. Do not submit payment information, credentials, secrets, health information, or other sensitive data.

Storage and access

Lead records must be stored privately outside the public site. Local/private operation may use append-only JSONL outside the web root. Netlify must use Forms or durable storage; Shopify must use approved customer/contact records or a private app/backend.

Product data

Before production launch, Jarv-ish will publish detailed terms for meeting recording consent, connected-agent data, retention, deletion, permissions, subprocessors, and customer controls.

Your choices

You may ask about access, correction, or deletion through the contact page. Final production contact details and jurisdiction-specific rights remain launch requirements.
